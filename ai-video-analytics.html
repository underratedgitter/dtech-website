Why AI video analytics?

Don't just record incidents. Prevent them.

Traditional CCTV only records events. AI video analytics actively detects abnormal activity, unauthorised access, suspicious behaviour and safety violations in real time, so security teams can respond before incidents escalate.

Intelligent detection capabilities

Six ways the system watches for you

Suspicious behaviour detection

Detect people behaving unusually or suspiciously.

  • Loitering
  • Repeated movement
  • Waiting in restricted zones
  • Abnormal movement patterns

Unauthorised intrusion detection

Instantly identify unauthorised entry into protected areas.

  • Perimeter fencing
  • Restricted production areas
  • Electrical substations
  • Storage yards

Absence detection

Alert when required personnel are missing from designated areas.

  • Security guard posts
  • Control rooms
  • Safety-critical workstations
  • Industrial operations

Overcrowding detection

Monitor occupancy and alert when predefined limits are exceeded.

  • Manufacturing plants
  • Railway stations
  • Airports
  • Shopping malls
  • Educational institutions

Vehicle monitoring

Monitor vehicle movement and detect:

  • Unauthorised vehicles
  • Stationary vehicles
  • Excessive parking duration
  • Loading and unloading delays

Tailgating detection

Detect people following authorised staff through secured entry points without authenticating.

  • Corporate offices
  • Data centres
  • Laboratories
  • Critical infrastructure
