How it works

From camera feed to action

01 / 03

Analyse camera feeds in real time

Our AI video monitoring uses advanced action recognition to detect incidents such as security breaches, theft, misplacement, spills, employee presence and SOP compliance.

02 / 03

Get actionable alerts, instantly

For EHS safety, when a potential incident is detected, key personnel receive an instant alert with the precise video footage, so they can respond swiftly and effectively.

03 / 03

Improve operational efficiency

Proactively monitor operations and machine maintenance, identify process improvements and track adherence to SOPs.
