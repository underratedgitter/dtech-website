D-TECH's AI Forklift Camera & Driver Monitoring System improves workplace safety and operational efficiency.

It combines front and rear vision cameras, AI-powered driver monitoring and real-time alerts to reduce accidents and improve operator awareness.

Key system features

Built to prevent the next near miss

  1. Real-time pedestrian detectionVisual and audible alerts when pedestrians enter danger zones, with adjustable sensitivity and detection range.
  2. AI driver monitoring (DMS)Alerts for fatigue, phone use, smoking and missing safety helmets, significantly reducing human error.
  3. Smart management systemOverspeed warnings, seat belt reminders and driver absence alarms.
