Who we are and what we do

A bridge between business needs and affordable, high-quality IT

D-TECH Solution Integrators connects what businesses need with dependable, affordable IT: servers, workstations, storage, desktops, laptops, networks, automation and the people who keep them running.

Mr. Bhavik Barot started the business in 1999 with commercial sales of servers, workstations, storage, desktops and laptops. It was incorporated as D-TECH Solution Integrators Private Limited in 2003 and has grown into a turnkey systems integrator for industrial IT, automation and managed services.
